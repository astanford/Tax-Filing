## Rules

### General mechanics (all three K-1 types)
- Keep the K-1 for your records; don't file it with your return unless specifically required (1065: box 15 code O backup withholding; 1120-S: box 13 code O; 1041: box 13 code B — attach a copy). [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 1; 1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 1; 1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 1]
- Loss limits apply in this order before anything reaches Schedule E: **basis limitations, at-risk limitations, passive activity limitations, then excess business loss limitations** (Form 461; disallowed amount becomes an NOL carryforward, not shown on Schedule E). [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 3; 1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 9; Schedule E Instructions (2025), pp. 8–9]
- Coded boxes: the entity prints a letter code left of the amount; "*" plus "STMT" means the detail is on an attached statement. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 14]
- Treating any item differently than the entity reported it requires Form 8082. [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 1; Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 8]

### K-1 (Form 1065) — Part II items that matter
- **Item D (PTP box):** if checked, you're in a publicly traded partnership — see PTP rules below. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 11]
- **Item J:** share of profit, loss, and capital percentages (beginning/ending), per the partnership agreement; "Sale"/"Exchange" checkboxes flag a transfer of your interest during the year. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 12]
- **Item K1:** your share of nonrecourse, qualified nonrecourse, and recourse liabilities — use the total of all three in figuring adjusted basis of your interest; generally only qualified nonrecourse financing and recourse amounts count as at-risk. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 12]
- **Item L:** capital account analysis reported on the **tax-basis method**. Caution: ending capital may NOT equal your adjusted tax basis (basis includes your share of liabilities; the capital account doesn't). You're responsible for maintaining your own basis records. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 12]

### Box 14 self-employment earnings — CRITICAL
- **Code A (net SE earnings):** enter on Schedule SE. If you're a **general partner**, first reduce it by any §179 deduction claimed, unreimbursed partnership expenses claimed, and depletion on oil/gas; don't reduce it by SE health insurance. If code A is a loss, enter only the deductible (post-limitation) amount on Schedule SE. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 21–22]
- Schedule E instructions confirm: part or all of partnership income may be SE earnings — "Enter the amount from Schedule K-1 (Form 1065), box 14, code A, on Schedule SE after you reduce this amount by any allowable expenses attributable to that income." [Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 9]
- **General vs. limited partner:** whether a partner is a limited partner *for SE-tax purposes* depends on §1402(a)(13), not the state-law label. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] The commonly stated corollary (limited partners include only guaranteed payments for services in SE earnings) is a partnership-side rule not stated in these PDFs — I cannot verify this — check IRS.gov (Form 1065 instructions, Schedule K line 14a).
- Practical effect: if box 14 is empty or zero (typical for a passive limited partner/investor member), nothing flows to Schedule SE from that K-1. If box 14A has an amount, Schedule SE is required. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 21–22]
- If both spouses are partners, each files their own Schedule SE. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 21]

### Box 20 code Z — §199A / QBI (the code that's almost always present)
- Deduction of up to 20% of net QBI plus 20% of qualified REIT dividends (§199A dividends) and qualified PTP income; the partnership attaches the detail (QBI, W-2 wages, UBIA). [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 29]
- Use **Form 8995** (simplified) only if: you have QBI/§199A dividends/PTP income; 2025 taxable income before the QBI deduction ≤ **$197,300 ($394,600 MFJ)**; and you aren't a patron of a specified agricultural/horticultural co-op. Otherwise use **Form 8995-A**. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 29–30]
- Same form-selection rule and thresholds apply to 1120-S box 17 code V and 1041 box 14 code I. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 17; 1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 4]

### Publicly traded partnerships (PTPs) — item D checked
- Passive limits apply **separately to each PTP**. Do NOT report PTP passive items on Form 8582. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 10]
- A net passive loss from a PTP can't offset other passive income: it is suspended and carried forward to offset **income from the same PTP** in later years. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 10]
- Overall gain from a PTP: the net-gain portion is nonpassive income (counts in MAGI and investment income); remaining income/losses report as passive. Enter "From PTP" next to the entries. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 10]
- Full disposition of the entire PTP interest to an unrelated person in a fully taxable transaction frees all suspended losses from the passive rules. [1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 10]

### K-1 (Form 1120-S) box-by-box (individual shareholder)
2025 box numbering from the form: 1–3 business/rental, 4–10 portfolio and other income, 11 §179, 12 other deductions, 13 credits, **14 = Schedule K-3 checkbox**, 15 AMT items, 16 items affecting shareholder basis, 17 other information, 18/19 multiple-activity checkboxes. There is no "other info in box 14" on the 1120-S K-1. [1120-S K-1 form (2025)]

- **S corporation income is never SE income:** "Your share of S corporation income isn't self-employment income and it isn't subject to self-employment tax." [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 1; Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 10]

| Box | Item | Where it goes |
|-----|------|---------------|
| 1 | Ordinary business income (loss) | Same passive/nonpassive routing as 1065 box 1 (Sch E line 28 cols (g)/(h)/(i)/(k), Form 8582 for passive losses), after basis and at-risk limits. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 6] |
| 2 | Net rental real estate income (loss) | Same as 1065 box 2, including the $25K active-participation direct-entry conditions. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 6–7] |
| 3 | Other net rental income (loss) | Passive for all shareholders; loss → Form 8582 → col (g); income → col (h). [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 7] |
| 4 | Interest income | Form 1040 line 2b. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 7] |
| 5a / 5b | Ordinary / qualified dividends | Line 3b / line 3a. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 7] |
| 6 | Royalties | Schedule E line 4. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 7] |
| 7 / 8a | Net short-term / long-term capital gain (loss) | Schedule D lines 5 / 12, after loss limitations. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 7] |
| 8b / 8c | Collectibles (28%) / unrecaptured §1250 gain | Same Schedule D worksheets as the 1065 boxes 9b/9c. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 7] |
| 9 | Net §1231 gain (loss) | Generally passive if from a rental or non-materially-participated business. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 7–8] |
| 10 | Other income (loss), coded | Statement-driven (e.g., code A other portfolio income). [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 8] |
| 11 | §179 deduction | Form 4562 Part I; passive → Form 8582; nonpassive → Sch E line 28 col (j), after basis/at-risk limits. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 9] |
| 12 | Other deductions, coded | Codes A–G charitable contributions (statement) → Sch A; code H investment interest expense → Form 4952 line 1 (pairs with box 17 codes A/B). [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 10–11] |
| 16 | Items affecting shareholder basis | Code A tax-exempt interest → 1040 line 2a, increases stock basis; code B other tax-exempt income increases basis; code C nondeductible expenses decrease basis; **code D distributions** — reduce stock basis; distributions in excess of stock basis are capital gain on Form 8949/Schedule D. Code E loan repayments (reduced-basis loans part income). [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 15] |
| 17 | Other information, coded | Code A investment income → Form 4952 line 4a; code B investment expenses → Form 4952 line 5; **code V = §199A/QBI** (see QBI section above). [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 15, 17] |

- **Actual cash distributions aren't on the K-1:** dividend distributions of $10+ (prior C-corp earnings and profits) come on Form 1099-DIV and go on Form 1040 line 3b. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 1; Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 9]

### Form 7203 (S corporation basis statement) — when it must be attached
- Stock basis adjusts annually in order: up for all income (including tax-exempt); down (not below zero) for box 16 code D distributions, then nondeductible expenses, then losses/deductions. Use Form 7203 to figure aggregate stock and debt basis. [1120-S K-1 Instructions (2025), pp. 2–3]
- **Attach trigger (printed on Schedule E):** "If you report a loss, receive a distribution, dispose of stock, or receive a loan repayment from an S corporation, you must check the box in column (e) on line 28 and attach the required basis computation." [Schedule E form (2025), p. 2; Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 10]
- The instructions name Form 7203 as that basis computation ("check the box ... column (e), and attach Form 7203 to your return"; note the instructions' text says "Part III, column (e)" but the basis checkbox is printed in Part II, line 28, column (e) — the form controls). [Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 8; Schedule E form (2025), p. 2]

Estates and trusts do NOT go on Schedule E Part II. Boxes 6–8 are reported on **Schedule E, line 33 (Part III)**, column (d) or (f). [1041 K-1 form (2025), p. 2]

| Box | Item | Where it goes |
|-----|------|---------------|
| 1 | Interest income | Form 1040 line 2b (and Sch B Part I if applicable). [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] |
| 2a / 2b | Ordinary / qualified dividends | Line 3b / line 3a. [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] |
| 3 / 4a | Net short-term / long-term capital gain | Schedule D lines 5 / 12. [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] |
| 4b / 4c | 28% rate gain / unrecaptured §1250 gain | 28% Rate Gain Worksheet line 4 / Unrecaptured §1250 Worksheet line 11. [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] |
| 5 | Other portfolio and nonbusiness income | Royalties, annuities, IRD, etc., not subject to passive rules → Schedule E line 33 col (f). [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2; 1041 K-1 form (2025), p. 2] |
| 6 / 7 / 8 | Ordinary business / net rental real estate / other rental income | Schedule E line 33, column (d) or (f); fiduciary provides a per-activity schedule. Losses in boxes 6–8 may be limited by §469, but "the rules for applying these limitations to beneficiaries haven't yet been issued." [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2; 1041 K-1 form (2025), p. 2] |
| 9 | Directly apportioned deductions (codes A–C: depreciation, depletion, amortization) | Form 8582 or Schedule E line 33 col (c) or (e). [1041 K-1 form (2025), p. 2; 1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] |
| 10 | Estate tax deduction (IRD) | Schedule A line 16. [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 2] |
| 11 | Final-year deductions | Only on the entity's final return: code A excess deductions—§67(e) expenses → Schedule 1 line 24k; code B non-misc itemized → Schedule A; codes C/D unused short/long capital loss carryovers → Schedule D lines 5/12; code E NOL carryover → Schedule 1 line 8a; code F ATNOL → Form 6251 line 2f. | 12 | AMT items (one line) | Code A adjustment → Form 6251 line 2j. [1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 3] |

| 14 | Other information | Code A tax-exempt interest → 1040 line 2a; code B foreign taxes → Schedule 3 line 1 or Schedule A line 6; code H §1411 NII adjustment → Form 8960 line 7; **code I = §199A/QBI** (see QBI section above). [1041 K-1 form (2025), p. 2; 1041 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 4] |

### Schedule E Part II mechanics (lines 27–32)
- **Line 27:** answer "Yes" if reporting (a) prior-year losses newly allowed after basis/at-risk limits — enter on a separate line 28 in col (i) with "PYA" in col (a); (b) prior-year unallowed passive losses not reported on Form 8582 — separate line, col (g), "PYA" in col (a); or (c) unreimbursed partnership expenses (§162, required by the partnership agreement) — separate line, col (i), "UPE" in col (a). [Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 10]
- **Line 28 columns as printed on the 2025 form:** (a) name; (b) enter **P** for partnership, **S** for S corporation; (c) check if foreign partnership; (d) EIN; (e) check if basis computation is required; (f) check if any amount is not at risk; then Passive Income and Loss — (g) passive loss allowed (attach Form 8582 if required), (h) passive income from Schedule K-1 — and Nonpassive Income and Loss — (i) nonpassive loss allowed (see Schedule K-1), (j) §179 expense deduction from Form 4562, (k) nonpassive income from Schedule K-1. [Schedule E form (2025), p. 2]
- Use one line per entity for current-year ordinary income/loss; each separately stated K-1 item goes in the applicable column of its **own line below**, with a description in col (a). Multiple K-1s: a separate line 28 entry per partnership. [Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 10; 1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 14]
- At-risk: check col (f) and use Form 6198 for losses with amounts not at risk. Passive losses generally need Form 8582 before col (g); passive income → col (h); nonpassive items → cols (i)–(k). [Schedule E Instructions (2025), pp. 8–9]
- **Lines 29a/29b:** totals of the income columns / loss columns. **Line 30** = add cols (h) and (k) of 29a; **line 31** = add cols (g), (i), (j) of 29b (entered as a loss); **line 32** = combine 30 and 31. [Schedule E form (2025), p. 2]
- Part III (estates/trusts): line 33 cols (a) name, (b) EIN; passive — (c) passive deduction or loss allowed (attach Form 8582 if required), (d) passive income from Schedule K-1; nonpassive — (e) deduction or loss from Schedule K-1, (f) other income from Schedule K-1; lines 34a/34b totals, 35 + 36 combine to 37. [Schedule E form (2025), p. 2]
- Line 41 (Part V) totals lines 26, 32, 37, 39, 40 and carries to **Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 5**. [Schedule E form (2025), p. 2]
- **"Losses not allowed":** apply basis rules (partnership: adjusted basis of interest, per the worksheet in the 1065 K-1 instructions; S corp: stock + loan basis, Form 7203), then at-risk (Form 6198), then passive (Form 8582); disallowed amounts carry forward. [Schedule E Instructions (2025), pp. 8–9]
- **PTP special rule on Schedule E:** the Schedule E instructions defer to the K-1 instructions for PTP items ("See the Schedule K-1 instructions before entering on your return ... income or loss from any publicly traded partnership."). PTP losses are not entered on Form 8582; report suspended-loss netting per the PTP rules above, marking entries "From PTP." [Schedule E Instructions (2025), p. 9; 1065 K-1 Instructions (2025), p. 10]
